# 3-minute demo script

1. **Problem (20 s):** Most household and campus waste is mixed, so recyclable material and its value are lost.
2. **Login (10 s):** Enter your name. The dashboard greets you by name.
3. **Scan (60 s):** Scan Waste, upload a photo of one item, tap Detect with AI, confirm the category, enter weight, Analyze and save. Show the price, or the "recycle, do not throw it outside" message (English and Hindi) when there is no resale price.
4. **Dashboard (30 s):** Show totals, the eco score and badges, then download the impact card.
5. **Scale (30 s):** Energy & Impact, "What if more people join?" Set 500 people and show the yearly projection.
6. **Local action (20 s):** Collection Route shows real Panipat recyclers on Google Maps. Marketplace lets people list sorted waste.
7. **Honest close (10 s):** Rates and energy values are indicative. Next steps: a shared database, verified recyclers and calibrated energy data.

Before presenting: save your Gemini key in AI settings, test one photo, keep a screen recording as backup.
